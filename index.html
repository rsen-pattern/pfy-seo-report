📈

Strong Growth Trajectory

Organic traffic has grown from ~3K/mo (May 2024) to 33.6K/mo — a 10× increase in 24 months. The site is clearly in a growth phase.

🔍

Predominantly Non-Branded

97% of organic traffic is non-branded, meaning the site earns traffic from generic supplement keywords — a sign of strong topical authority.

🎯

Informational Intent Dominates

83% of keywords carry informational intent. This indicates an opportunity to push more commercial and transactional content to convert existing traffic.

📉

Paid Traffic Declining

Paid traffic dropped sharply from ~69K (Jan 2025) to ~12.5K (May 2026). Budget has been scaled back — organic must close this gap.

⚠️

Low Authority Link Profile

80% of referring domains have an authority score of 0–10. Quality over quantity focus needed — building links from AS 40+ publishers should be a priority.

✅

Strong Editorial Links Exist

Quality links from Healthline, Men's Health, Medical News Today, Vogue, and The Good Trade demonstrate the brand earns editorial coverage from high-authority health media.

🌎

Link Geography

50% of linking domains are US-based, with 33% from Singapore — the Singapore cluster is largely low-quality directory/aggregator sites to audit.

⚓

Anchor Text Profile

Top anchors: "consumer site" (16%), brand URL (10%), "take me to pure for you" (7%). Branded anchor text concentration is healthy — no over-optimised money anchors.
